## Core Hackathon Themes
ETHPrague strongly focuses on **Solarpunk, public goods, sustainability, localism, and decentralized infrastructure** that solves real-world human coordination problems. Submissions should emphasize positive sum games and resilient technology.

## Target Bounties & Tracks
Our project is designed to hit these specific tracks. Refer back to these when writing the final submission README:

1. **SpaceComputer**: Orbital KMS for secure settlement and cTRNG for verifiable randomness.
2. **ENS**: Seamless identity resolution and programmatic subname issuance.
3. **Blockscout**: Integration of the App SDK for rich, explorer-native transaction UX.
4. **Swarm**: Decentralized persistence layer for game records/data.
5. **Gensyn AXL**: P2P agentic workflows and decentralized AI compute.
6. **Privy**: Frictionless Web3 onboarding for non-crypto-native humans.
7. **Apify / Umia / Sourcify**: Data extraction, economic prize volume tracking, and fully verified smart contracts.
